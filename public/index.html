Finding PhD

US PhD programs from official university websites. Pick filters to narrow the list.

How to use

  1. Pick values in the filters on the left. "All" means no filter. The list, summary and chart update right away.
  2. Deadline on or after: hides programs with an earlier deadline. Programs with no known deadline stay.
  3. My TOEFL and My GPA: type your own score. Programs that ask for more disappear; programs with no stated minimum stay.
  4. Click a program row to see funding details, requirements, professors and the official pages used.

Not stated means the official website does not say; it was left blank instead of guessed. Funding Y means the website says PhD students receive assistantships or fellowships; "competitive" in the details means not every student gets one. (borderline fit) marks programs related to, but not exactly in, the field. Always confirm deadlines and stipends on the official page before applying.
